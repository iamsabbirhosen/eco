01 / Traditional Economy

Rooted in Custom
& Tradition

  • Decisions guided by long-standing customs and cultural beliefs
  • Occupations passed down through generations
  • Production mainly for self-consumption; simple technology
  • Trade through barter — goods exchanged without money
  • Change is slow; stability is the priority
🌾

02 / Command Economy

State-Controlled Production

🏛️

Centralised Planning

The government decides what to produce, how much, and at what price

🏭

State Ownership

Land, factories, and machines are owned and controlled by the state

⚖️

Equality Over Profit

Goal is equitable distribution, not individual profit or market gain

⚠️

Information Gap

No single authority can know everyone's needs — leads to inefficiency

🚫

Limited Freedom

Private businesses and individuals have little economic autonomy

🌍

Examples

Former Soviet Union & North Korea

03 / Market Economy

Driven by Demand
& Supply

  • Individuals & businesses make all major decisions
  • Prices determined by demand and supply
  • Rising prices signal scarcity, motivating production
  • Profit is the primary motivator
  • Minimal government involvement
  • Example: United States
